# 📊 Amazon Sales Analysis Dashboard | Power BI

> An interactive Business Intelligence dashboard developed using Microsoft Power BI to analyze Amazon product sales, customer reviews, product performance, and key business KPIs.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Amazon Sales Analysis Dashboard** is a Power BI business intelligence project designed to provide a clear and interactive view of sales performance.

The dashboard helps analyze:

- 📈 Year-to-Date (YTD) Sales
- 📅 Quarter-to-Date (QTD) Sales
- 📦 Products Sold
- ⭐ Customer Reviews
- 🛍️ Product Category Performance
- 🏆 Top 5 Products by Sales
- ⭐ Top 5 Products by Reviews
- 📆 Monthly Sales Trends
- 📊 Weekly Sales Performance

The project demonstrates the complete Power BI workflow, from connecting and cleaning data to creating DAX calculations, KPIs, interactive visuals, and dashboard navigation.

---

## 🎯 Project Objective

The primary objective of this project is to transform raw Amazon sales data into an interactive dashboard that helps users understand:

- Overall sales performance
- Monthly and weekly sales trends
- High-performing product categories
- Top-performing products
- Customer engagement through product reviews
- YTD and QTD business performance

# 📌 Key Performance Indicators

The dashboard includes the following major KPIs:

| KPI | Purpose |
|---|---|
| 💰 YTD Sales | Measures total year-to-date sales performance |
| 💵 QTD Sales | Measures quarterly sales performance |
| 📦 YTD Products Sold | Tracks the total number of products sold |
| ⭐ YTD Reviews | Measures total product reviews received |

# 📊 Dashboard Visualizations

## 1️⃣ YTD Sales by Month

**Chart Type:** Line Chart

Used to visualize monthly sales trends and identify:

- Growth patterns
- Seasonal trends
- Monthly fluctuations
- High and low performing months

---

## 2️⃣ YTD Sales by Week

**Chart Type:** Column Chart

Used to analyze weekly sales performance and identify short-term fluctuations in sales.

---

## 3️⃣ Sales by Product Category

**Chart Type:** Table / Heat Map

Provides a high-level comparison of sales across different product categories.

The analysis includes:

- YTD Sales
- QTD Sales
- % of YTD Sales

---

## 4️⃣ Top 5 Products by YTD Sales

**Chart Type:** Bar Chart

Highlights the five products generating the highest year-to-date sales.

This helps identify the major revenue-generating products.

---

## 5️⃣ Top 5 Products by YTD Reviews

**Chart Type:** Bar Chart

Highlights products with the highest number of reviews.

This provides an indication of customer engagement and product popularity.
